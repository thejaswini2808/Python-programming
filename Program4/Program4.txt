Title: Electricity Bill Generator
Objective
Develop a simple real-world billing application using Python variables, arithmetic operators, input/output statements, built-in functions, and formatted printing.
Problem Statement
A residential electricity provider wants a simple billing system.
Write a Python program that performs the following tasks:
1. Accept the following details from the user:
○ Consumer Name
○ Consumer ID
○ Previous Meter Reading (kWh)
○ Current Meter Reading (kWh)
○ Cost per Unit (₹)
2. Calculate:
○ Total Units Consumed
○ Energy Charge
○ Electricity Duty (5% of Energy Charge)
○ Fixed Meter Charge (₹100)
○ Net Bill Amount
3. Display the bill in a professional format.
Formula:
Units Consumed = Current Reading − Previous Reading
Energy Charge = Units × Cost per Unit
Electricity Duty = 5% of Energy Charge
Fixed Meter Charge = ₹100
Net Bill = Energy Charge + Electricity Duty + Fixed Charge
